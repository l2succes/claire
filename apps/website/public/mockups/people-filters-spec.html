How the menu works

1. Pick one People stateAll, Contacted, Needs context, or Groups are mutually exclusive.
2. Narrow by platformThe platform choice composes with the People state.
3. Keep intelligence honestFuture filters are visible but disabled until their data has a reliable source, a reason, and a resolution state.

Available now

  • All people — the linked account's synced contacts.
  • Contacted — direct people with at least one message sent by the account owner.
  • Needs context — non-group people without saved relationship context.
  • Groups — group conversations.
  • Platform — the People state above, limited to one network.

Open loops: required contract

A future open-loop entry must include a type, status, last activity, optional due date, source message reference, and a resolution action. It is not an unread count and it never silently guesses that a relationship is unhealthy.

COMING SOON until Claire can produce an explainable result and let the person mark it resolved.

Claire-specific follow-ons

  • Cross-network identity linking with confidence and manual correction.
  • Relationship preferences for tone, AI context, reminders, and notifications.
  • “Who should I talk to?” — an explainable shortlist based on open loops and chosen priorities.
  • Private recency and follow-up cues, never opaque relationship scores.

Implementation boundary

NOW Contacted is deterministic server-side metadata: a user-scoped contact appears only when there is an outbound direct message linked to that contact. The client receives people and message-presence only—never message text—to render this filter. LATER Open loops, Needs reply, Follow-ups due, Reconnecting, Context gaps, and Important moments require their own source-of-truth records, explicit lifecycle states, and user-visible evidence before they become active filters.
