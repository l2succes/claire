PRODUCT DECISIONS TO CARRY INTO BUILD

The design rules behind the screens.

Five primary tabs

Home, Inbox, Promises, and Search cover the daily loop. Contacts stays inside Inbox; Ask Claire sits at the center of the shell with its own visual space; Settings still opens from profile.

AI is a layer, not a destination

Ask Claire is a permanent center action for cross-chat questions. Claire also appears in the active conversation, a native sheet, search answers, and the daily brief.

Relationship categories

  • Business, client, colleague, mentor
  • Family, close friend, acquaintance
  • Dating, partner, former partner
  • Community, service provider, other

Required states beyond mockups

  • Empty, loading, offline, and sync-conflict states
  • Permission denied and platform disconnected
  • Message failed, retrying, and partially synced
  • AI unavailable, low-confidence, and source missing
