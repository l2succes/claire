# Product roadmap

Claire is alpha. Current capabilities and planned work are intentionally separated.

## Current

- Unified inbox for WhatsApp, Telegram, and Instagram through Matrix
- Mobile Expo client and macOS desktop companion
- Promise detection, drafts, and smart cards
- Local mock mode for contributors
- Example calendar and task plugins with fixtures

## Planned

- Broader connector catalog (see `packages/platform-catalog`)
- Stronger privacy claims after evidence gates
- Public plugin review and marketplace
- History rewrite after credential rotation

See [docs/PLATFORM_CONNECTOR_ROADMAP.md](../PLATFORM_CONNECTOR_ROADMAP.md) and [docs/SECURITY_CLAIMS_AND_ROADMAP.md](../SECURITY_CLAIMS_AND_ROADMAP.md).
