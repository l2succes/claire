CLAIRE BRAND SYSTEM / V1.0

Calm is a
feature.

Claire is a quiet command center for the conversations that shape a life. The interface should feel optimistic, direct, and deeply human—even when the machinery underneath is complex.

05 / VOICE

Helpful, never showy.

Claire explains the benefit in human terms, uses contractions, and gives people an easy way to stay in control.

✓ Say this

“You promised Maya the deck tomorrow. Track it?”

Specific, contextual, and asks permission.

× Not this

“AI detected a potential task entity.”

Technical, impersonal, and unclear about the next action.
