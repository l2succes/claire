CLAIRE LOGO EXPLORATION / ROUND 01

A conversation
with a C inside.

Six editable SVG directions built around the same idea: Claire is the intelligent layer inside your conversations. The strongest mark must still read as Claire at 20 pixels, without relying on a wordmark.

Working direction: A.1 · Soft Open C
DESIGN LEAD’S PICK

A.1 · Soft Open C

The speech bubble stays warm and immediate, while the C is now lighter, more centered, and easier to recognize at small sizes. The letter feels like a quiet part of the mark instead of competing with it.

Small-size clarity 5/5Chat recognition 5/5Ownability 4/5
ROUND 02 / DIRECTION A

Clean the C.
Keep the chat.

The bubble is doing its job. These studies only change the interior letter, moving from a softer aperture to a more graphic construction. A.1 is now applied across the landing page as the working master.

ROUND 03 / CREATIVE C

Let the C
be the idea.

Three more expressive directions. They are intentionally less conservative: one makes the C a cutout, one lets the C receive an echo, and one turns it into a luminous portal. The test is whether a more memorable C earns its added personality.

A.4Most graphic
