A conversation
with a C inside.

Six editable SVG directions built around the same idea: Claire is the intelligent layer inside your conversations. The strongest mark must still read as Claire at 20 pixels, without relying on a wordmark.

Recommended starting point: A · Open C
DESIGN LEAD’S PICK

A · Open C

It says “chat” immediately, keeps the C legible at small sizes, and feels warm without looking childish. The open counter also hints at Claire bringing separate networks into one place.
