What “local” means today

Self-hosting keeps Claire’s message store on infrastructure you control. The original messaging networks still process their messages, and configured external AI providers may receive selected conversation content. We will not claim “never stored in the cloud” until desktop-only mode has passed its security and network-egress review.

* iMessage needs its Mac host available. Google Messages depends on the paired Android phone.

SELF-HOSTED

Run Claire yourself

Best when you want to operate the existing Docker stack on hardware or infrastructure you control.

  1. 1Clone Claire and configure Supabase, Matrix, Redis, and bridges.
  2. 2Choose your model provider and keep its credentials in your host environment.
  3. 3Run the Docker stack and keep that host available for connected bridges.

You can use Claire Desktop for setup in either mode. It authorizes a connection; it is not automatically the machine that runs the bridge.

HOW AI IS BILLED

Choose who pays for the model.

Messages remain usable when an AI allowance is empty. Claire will never silently bill an unlimited model balance.

Claire AI credits

Use Claire-managed models for replies, Ask Claire, summaries, and search. The workspace gets a visible annual credit balance, warnings, and a hard cap.

Credits settle against actual model usage—not a made-up “one request” unit.

Bring your own key

Add an OpenAI, Anthropic, or compatible provider key. Claire Cloud still runs the product; your provider bills model usage directly.

Your Claire AI credit balance is not used.

Run models yourself

Self-hosted teams can use Ollama, LM Studio, or another compatible endpoint on infrastructure they control.

Available only when the model host stays reachable.

Before launch: the included credit amount, top-up price, and supported model tiers will be published in the order form. We will show usage and require an explicit approval before any additional credit purchase.

What happens when our Claire AI credits run out?

Messaging, search, and connections continue to work. AI actions pause until an administrator adds prepaid credits, switches to a workspace provider key, or waits for the next allowance period.

Can we use our own OpenAI or Anthropic account?

Yes. Your encrypted provider credential is used only for your workspace’s AI requests. You pay that provider directly, and Claire Cloud does not deduct managed credits for those requests.

Is $10,000 the price for each person?

No. It is the annual price for one managed Claire workspace. Seat limits, included AI credits, and support terms belong in the signed order form rather than being hidden in usage billing.
